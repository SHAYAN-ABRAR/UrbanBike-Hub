How it works

Browse now, pick up where you left off

There are no accounts and nothing to sign up for. The only thing this site saves is your cart, and it saves it on your own browser.

  1. 1

    Browse the catalog

    Search by name or colour, or filter by style. Open any bike to see a larger photo and what it shows.

  2. 2

    Add bikes to your cart

    Pick a quantity, up to five of each bike. Adding a bike doesn't order it or reserve it.

  3. 3

    Come back to it later

    Your cart is kept in this browser's local storage, so it's still there after you close the page. It isn't sent anywhere.

Frequently asked questions

About this site

Is UrbanBike Hub a real shop?

No. It's a demo website for browsing motorbikes. The bike names, descriptions and prices are illustrative, and nothing here can be bought or reserved.

Where is my cart saved?

Only in this browser, in its local storage. It isn't sent to a server, so it won't show up on another browser or device, and clearing this site's data empties it.

Can I check out or pay?

No. There's no checkout and no payment system. Use the cart to keep track of the bikes you like.

Why aren't there any specifications?

Each bike is described only from what its photo shows. This demo has no verified specifications, so it doesn't list any.

Riding basics

What should I know before riding a motorbike?

Get proper training and the licence your country requires. Wear a helmet that fits and meets your local safety standard, along with gloves, boots and protective clothing.

Before each ride, check the tyres, brakes, lights and controls. On the road, ride as if other drivers haven't seen you: keep a safe distance, stay out of blind spots and follow the traffic laws.

How can I tell if my helmet needs replacing?

Replace it after any crash or hard knock, even if it looks fine, because the foam liner inside is designed to absorb a single impact. Also replace it if the shell is cracked, the liner is crumbling or dented, it no longer fits snugly, or the strap or buckle is worn.

Many helmet makers suggest replacing a helmet about every five years even without damage. Check the advice in your helmet's manual.

My bike has been in storage. Is it safe to ride?

Check it over first. Look at the tyres for the right pressure, cracks or flat spots, charge or test the battery, check the oil, brake fluid and coolant, and make sure the chain or belt is lubricated and correctly adjusted. Then test the brakes, lights and horn.

Fuel left in the tank for months can go stale. If anything looks wrong, or you're not sure, have a qualified mechanic check the bike before you ride it.
